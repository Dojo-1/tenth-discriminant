DETERMINANT STRUCTURE / LIFTS -- finite experiment, before running it

Question: does retaining row correlation improve the geometric upper
bound, and what do exact prime-power layers say about valuation slack?
This is not an all-dimensional theorem or a new class-number proof.

Fixed campaign:
 D in {7,19,23,43,67,163}; 23 is a non-class-number-one control.
 Points: the 3 by 3 grid and the exact ellipse 4(x^2+D*y^2)<=9D.
 (R,S) in {(4,2),(6,2),(9,3)}; J in {1,2,L+M}.
 L is the maximum number of points on a horizontal line; M its line count.
 Use the existing integer trace-Gram construction without changing it.

For each case report exact rank and the sufficient independence test
 J>=L+M and nJ>2RS+J(R+S). Its failure does not imply dependence.
 Singular cases have no finite log determinant or determinant valuation.
 For nonsingular cases compare the Green inert-prime lower bound with:
 exact determinant, ordinary Hadamard, aggregated upper_B, and a product
 of disjoint principal blocks of size at most two (Fischer inequality).
 Greedily choose pairs by their exact block/diagonal-product ratio;
 this is a deterministic heuristic, not a globally optimal partition.
 Compare logarithmic gaps and gains using 160-bit Arb, not float decisions.

Prime-power lift means the Smith filtration of this integer Gram matrix:
 if e_i=v_p(s_i) for Smith factors s_i, let c_k=#{i:e_i>=k}.
 Then sum_k c_k=v_p(det G). The decreasing layer counts describe ONE
 valuation, not extra independent divisibility. Check product |s_i|=det G
 and each Green bound v_p(det G)>=2F(p) for actual odd inert p<=R.
 No extrapolation from measured surplus to a universal stronger bound.

Adversarial controls: singular matrices and failed independence,
 overlapping/incomplete block partitions, the prime 2 excluded from Green,
 nested-layer accounting, deliberately overstrong valuation and upper
 bounds, and a correlated positive definite example. Check derivative
 saturation at J>=R. Genuine h=1 fields must not give contradictions.
 Compare witnesses separately; do not add their bounds or valuations.
 Field extensions are excluded: no class-number-preserving bridge exists
 in this experiment. Larger modulus layers are not new field extensions.

Pass: all exact inequalities hold and controls catch their mutations.
 Record any failure, including unsupported improvement claims. A finite
 gain is useful evidence for a later analytic bound, not such a bound.
