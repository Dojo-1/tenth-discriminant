RADIAL CORRELATIONS -- registered before the campaign

Goal: replace observed full-matrix pairing by explicit moment formulas
and a uniform analytic correlation bound for a specified annular family.
Do not modify Ender, its certificates, or its asymptotic slope.

Rows (r,s) and (r+1,s+1) have matching phase at each derivative column.
Their diagonal and cross entries can be computed from M_k=sum |z|^(2k)
without forming a full RS by RS matrix. Verify those formulas against
the existing independent full-Gram construction on small point sets,
including origins, asymmetric sets and derivative orders above r+1.

Proposed uniform lemma: for a<=|z|^2<=b, a>0, and r>=J-1, the second
row equals the first times a positive column-dependent factor lambda in
[a,b*(r+1)/(r+2-J)]. The weighted Kantorovich inequality bounds the
squared correlation below by 4K/(1+K)^2, K=b*(r+1)/(a*(r+2-J)).
In particular, a>=b/2 and r>=2(J-1) give K<=4 and a two-row block ratio
at most 9/25. Prove this before calling it an analytic improvement.
Pair rows lexicographically, without overlap, only in this safe regime.
Keep unpaired rows as singletons. Require the existing sufficient
independence conditions before advertising a positive determinant bound.
If they fail, record "no certificate", not singular or contradictory.

Fixed campaign: D in {19,43,163}, outer t<=36D, inner 2t>=36D,
integer points (x,y), t=x^2+D*y^2; R in {64,128,256}, S in {2,4};
J=L+M from exact line counts. Record failures and zero eligible pairs.
For certified cases compare exact diagonal and moment-block products,
the uniform diagonal*(9/25)^m bound, and Green's actual-inert-prime
product. Report normalized logarithmic gaps, not raw dimension as gain.
No full Gram matrix, determinant or Smith form in the larger campaign.

Adversarial controls: wrong cross moments; derivative-boundary extra
terms; origin not satisfying annulus; r below the safe derivative range;
overlapping blocks; missing rank guard; and a strengthened correlation
bound contradicted by an explicit positive definite example.
Check moment bounds against full determinants on small certified cases.

Crucial limitation to establish: the guaranteed saving m*log(25/9)
is at most RS*log(25/9)/2; after division by R^2*S it is O(1/R).
This guarantees a finite saving but cannot itself change the leading
large-D slope. It is NOT an upper bound on the actual moment saving.
Changing a disk to an annulus also costs points; do not compare those
as if they were the same witness or claim an improved global cutoff.
