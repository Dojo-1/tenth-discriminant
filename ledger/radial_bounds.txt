RADIAL CORRELATIONS -- analytic lemma and moment-only bounds

Status: derived mathematical argument with finite exact controls, not a
formal proof or a human-reviewed theorem. Ender is unchanged. Preregistered
in flow/PREREG_RADIAL.txt; data in ledger/radial_bounds.jsonl.

1. Exact identities, no angular averaging

Let P be a finite set of distinct integer points z=x+y*sqrt(-D), D>0.
Write t=|z|^2=x^2+D*y^2 and M_k=sum_(z in P) t^k, including M_0=|P|.
The existing trace Gram construction uses row
 u_(r,s)(z,j)=(r)_j*z^(r-j)*conjugate(z)^s, 0<=j<J,
with (r)_j=r!/(r-j)! when j<=r, otherwise zero.
Its real inner product is 2 Re sum u*conjugate(v).
For every finite P, including nonsymmetric sets and the origin,
 G_(r,s),(r,s)=2 sum_(j<=min(r,J-1)) (r)_j^2 M_(r+s-j),
 G_(r,s),(r+1,s+1)
   =2 sum_(j<=min(r,J-1)) (r)_j (r+1)_j M_(r+s+1-j).
This follows by multiplying the powers explicitly: the cross term is
|z|^(2(r+s+1-j)). No assumption of continuous circular symmetry is used.
The diagonal of (r+1,s+1) includes its j=r+1 term if J>=r+2;
that term must NOT be dropped merely because the cross entry lacks it.

These formulas evaluate the selected principal blocks and all diagonals
from R+S-1 moments. They do not construct a full Gram matrix, its rank,
its determinant or Smith factors. Exact sufficient independence checks
must therefore precede certification.

2. An analytic correlation estimate

Suppose 0<a<=t<=b for every point, and r>=J-1. The pair
u=u_(r,s), v=u_(r+1,s+1) satisfies, in every column,
 v=lambda*u, lambda=t*(r+1)/(r+1-j).
There is no unmatched derivative column because j<J<=r+1.
Thus a<=lambda<=B=b*(r+1)/(r+2-J).
The same positive lambda multiplies both real coordinates in the trace
inner product. Normalize their nonnegative squared u-coordinates into
weights with sum one. Then
 corr(u,v)^2=(E lambda)^2/E(lambda^2).
Since (lambda-a)(lambda-B)<=0,
 E(lambda^2)<=(a+B)*mu-a*B, mu=E lambda.
Moreover
 ((a+B)*mu-a*B)/mu^2 <= (a+B)^2/(4*a*B);
after multiplying through this is
 ((a+B)*mu-2*a*B)^2>=0.
Consequently corr^2>=4K/(1+K)^2, K=B/a>=1.
The 2x2 block determinant divided by its diagonal product is therefore
at most ((K-1)/(K+1))^2.

If a>=b/2 and r>=2(J-1), then
 (r+1)/(r+2-J)<=2, hence K<=4 and the block ratio is <=9/25.
This also covers J=1. If K=1 the two rows are proportional, so they
cannot both occur in a positive definite full Gram matrix. The uniform
9/25 bound remains a valid, deliberately weaker statement.

Take any DISJOINT collection of m such pairs; all remaining rows are
singleton blocks. For a positive definite G, Fischer's inequality gives
 det G <= exact moment-block product <= Hadamard*(9/25)^m.
The implementation verifies both the parameter-specific rational bound
and the uniform 9/25 bound using exact integers. Its partition is a
fixed lexicographic rule, not an assertion of optimal pairing.

3. Independence, arithmetic and comparison scope

For the actual selected point set count L=max points on a horizontal
line, M=number of such lines, n=|P|. Require
 J>=L+M and nJ>2RS+J(R+S).
The inherited interpolation lemma then supplies nonvanishing; trace
Gram positivity supplies det G>0. Failure is "no certificate", not a
proof of singularity. Annular deletion preserves integrality but does
not automatically preserve independence or point-count estimates from
the full disk. The line and point counts are recomputed exactly.

Let Q=prod_(odd inert p<=R) p^(2F(p)), with inertness computed for the
actual D, not asserted from an h=1 hypothesis. The inherited valuation
lemma gives Q<=det G. The campaign checks Q<=moment-block product.
There is no combination of prime-power layers or different witnesses.
No annulus bound is installed into Ender's all-D proof or cutoff.

4. What this guarantee can and cannot buy

Its guaranteed logarithmic saving is m*log(25/9), with m<=RS/2.
After the existing normalization by R^2*S it is at most
 log(25/9)/(2R), which tends to zero.
Thus THIS GUARANTEE cannot change the leading log(D) slope as R grows.
It can sharpen a finite upper bound. This is NOT an upper bound on the
actual moment-based saving, nor an impossibility theorem for larger
blocks, better pair estimates or other sampling sets.
Changing a disk to an annulus also removes points. A block saving on
the new witness is not by itself a net improvement on the disk witness.

5. Fixed campaign and observations

For D in {19,43,163}, use 18D<=x^2+D*y^2<=36D;
R in {64,128,256}, S in {2,4}, J=L+M.
All endpoints and points are integers. No full matrix is used here.
The 18 registered cases yield:
 - 16 certified by the sufficient independence conditions;
 - 2 uncertified (D=19, R=256, both S), recorded without reinterpretation;
 - 8 certified with eligible pairs and strict saving;
 - 8 certified with no eligible pairs, hence no saving.
The maximum row count is 1024; not all largest cases certify.
For both failed cases n=228<R+S (258 or 260); the sufficient condition
nJ>2RS+J(R+S) is therefore impossible for EVERY positive J on that
fixed point set. Adjusting J alone cannot repair this particular guard.
This does not prove actual row dependence.

For example D=19,R=128,S=2,J=42 has 228 points and 45 disjoint pairs.
Normalized gain is about .00140302447934 guaranteed and .0108528954116
from exact moments. Normalized gap above Q drops from 3.42442912074
(Hadamard) to 3.41357622533 (moment bound). The gap stays positive:
this is NOT a contradiction for the genuine h=1 field D=19.
Every registered case has strict_cutoff=false: R is above the
Rabinowitsch range, so using ONLY actually inert primes is essential.
No improved finite discriminant cutoff is claimed.

Nine unittest controls pass. Moment diagonals and cross entries match
the existing full-Gram construction on ellipses, asymmetric point sets,
grids and derivative-boundary cases. They include explicit mutations
of cross moments and unjustified derivative-range/annulus assumptions.
The analytic ratio is checked against an explicit two-row example
that rejects a stronger bound. Disjoint partitions, failed rank guards,
exact annulus enumeration and zero-pair behavior are checked.
A certified D=1 algebraic control (R=40,S=2,J=20, 80 rows) with a
nontrivial pair compares the moment bound to the full exact determinant.
D=1 here labels Z[i] in the algebraic control; it is NOT asserted to be
the absolute fundamental discriminant, which would be 4.

Existing 12 lift controls and the full Formic suite also pass, including
250 Ender intervals and its unchanged universal-envelope margin.
Shared code and finite controls do not independently establish Green's
lemmas, formalization or human mathematical review.
