DETERMINANT STRUCTURE / LIFTS -- finite research record

Scope
Implemented the fixed grid in flow/PREREG_LIFTS.txt. These are exact
small-matrix observations and general linear-algebra identities, not a
new class-number proof or a theorem about arbitrary dimensions/lifts.
The Ender bounds and certificates are unchanged.

Definitions and justified relationships
Use the existing derivative trace Gram matrix in Z[sqrt(-D)]. It is an
integer positive semidefinite matrix, with RS rows. Exact rank determines
whether det G>0. The sufficient interpolation conditions are reported
separately; failure of those conditions does not assert dependence.

For nonsingular G, Smith factors s_i satisfy s_i | s_(i+1) and
prod |s_i|=det G. At an odd inert prime p, write e_i=v_p(|s_i|).
The layer c_k=#{i:e_i>=k} describes the Smith filtration modulo p^k:
 |ker(G mod p^k)|=p^(sum_i min(e_i,k)).
 Thus c_k is the increment in log_p kernel size between levels k-1 and k,
 and sum_k c_k=v_p(det G). These are nested contributions to ONE
 valuation. Adding k*c_k instead would double-count.
The layer identity is checked against independently enumerated modular
kernels for a small correlated matrix. The general identity follows
because unimodular Smith transformations are invertible modulo p^k.

For a positive definite matrix and a partition into principal blocks,
Fischer gives det G <= prod det G_block <= prod G_ii.
One justification for two complementary blocks A,C: the Schur complement
C-B^T A^(-1) B is positive definite and <=C, so its determinant is <=det C.
Induct on the number of blocks. Each 2x2 block has determinant
G_ii*G_jj-G_ij^2 <= G_ii*G_jj.
The implementation chooses disjoint pairs by ascending exact rational
block/diagonal ratio and leaves singletons. It is deterministic but not
a claim of optimal matching. Overlapping or incomplete partitions fail.

For EACH witness separately, compute
 Q=prod_(odd inert p<=R) p^(2F(p)).
Check Q<=det G<=block product<=diagonal product and
log(diagonal product)<=upper_B. The exact integer inequalities, not
rounded printed decimals, establish the first chain. Arb checks the
aggregate comparison. Output gaps are (log(bound)-log Q)/(R^2*S);
smaller is better. Block gain is log(diagonal product/block)/(R^2*S).
Output contains actual inert primes, not a hypothetical assertion that
all primes are inert. strict_cutoff reports 4R<D+1 separately.

Outcomes
ledger/determinant_lifts.jsonl records 108 cases:
 101 positive determinants; 7 singular, with no finite logdet/valuation;
 23 satisfy the sufficient independence test;
 78 are positive without satisfying that sufficient test;
 all 101 positive cases have a strictly improved block bound;
 all checked Green valuation bounds and exact chains pass.
No cross-witness valuations or determinants were added.
D=23 is a non-h1 arithmetic control, not a candidate h1 field.

An instructive obstruction to a universal valuation improvement:
 D=19, ellipse 4(x^2+19*y^2)<=171, R=4, S=2, J=16.
There are 31 points and 8 independent rows. Both the sufficient rank
condition and strict inertness cutoff hold. At p=3, Green's 2F(3)=2
and the actual v_3(det G)=2, with layer list [2].
Thus a universal replacement 2F(p)+2 is FALSE even on this guarded,
genuine h=1 control. This does not rule out improvements with extra
large-D or parameter hypotheses.
Here the normalized gaps are approximately
 exact 2.39681460759, block 2.39952131924,
 Hadamard 2.58987912476, aggregate 3.44802697801.
The block saving is about .190357805521. These illustrative decimals
summarize the certified balls saved in the JSONL, not new proof inputs.

Higher derivatives saturate: once J>=R, extra columns vanish identically
because j>r for every monomial row. A control checks the unchanged Gram
matrix at J=R and J=R+1. Increasing the printed aggregate bound beyond
this point must not be confused with new actual matrix information.

Limits / next mathematical obligation
Smith computation measures valuations AFTER constructing a small matrix;
it is not an efficient large-D certificate or a priori stronger bound.
A sampled block saving does not prove an asymptotic saving. The next
obligation would be an analytic correlation estimate for a specified
family, preserving independence and integrality. Field-extension lifts
remain out of scope: no preservation of class number one is assumed.
Shared Gram code means the experiment is not an independent rederivation
of Green's determinant lemmas. Finite controls and AI review are not
formal verification or human mathematical review.
