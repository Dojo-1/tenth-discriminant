## Phase134: verified obstruction, corrected transport target (30 October)

[PR #52](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/pull/52) non-destructively merged [the Lean all-maximal-minors divisibility theorem](lean/TenthDiscriminant/RankDivisorCompatibility.lean) and the positive rectangular-weight guard. The [exact-source full-root Lean, audit and negative-control run](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/actions/runs/41108320303) passed. The rejected first elaboration and its proof-only correction remain in PR history. This is a **new formally checked compatibility lemma**, not a numerical improvement on the accepted 47.0-billion discriminant theorem.

**Self-correction:** `U_p E = Δ_p Ehat`, `F(p)>0` makes every maximal minor of the **unnormalized** E vanish modulo p. It does **not** establish that `Ehat mod p` lacks full rank. External approaches may use precisely such row-normalized matrices, additional column scaling and a different triangular support. A comparison must therefore transport the full triple (observation support, row normalization, jet/column normalization), with exact integrality and factorial/prime guards. The [source-aware Tao-alignment discussion](research/tao_goal_alignment/FOLDING_TO_TAO_20261030.md) records this limitation and keeps original external attribution.

**Folded priority:** rather than another decimal cutoff or a 360×360 geometric display, pursue a small normal-form interface and a matched cost comparison: does the normalized observation matrix support a nonzero minor with an independently justified prime, and can the result simplify or strengthen our *same original Gram* proof? Kill any proposed transport which changes E without rebuilding both estimates, assumes the prime's existence circularly, divides by a factorial zero modulo p, or promotes a single minor's upper bound into a Gram upper bound. [Open implementation scope #51](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/issues/51); [independent human review #50](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/issues/50). No external message has been sent by this agent.

## Phase135 WordPress field: prime-adic Smith layers and Archimedean metric

[Tao's October 19 Gram-covolume sketch](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/19/a-sketch-of-the-proof-of-non-existence-of-siegel-zeroes/) and [October 20 challenge](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/20/an-experimental-ai-math-challenge-using-tenth-discriminant-bounds-to-explore-arguments-ruling-out-siegel-zeroes/) motivate a bounded [local Smith / covolume transport note](research/tao_goal_alignment/WORDPRESS_LOCAL_SMITH_COVOLUME_20261030.md). This does **not** import the external argument or claim originality of determinant divisors. Under the existing *same original* integral row identity `U E = Delta_p C`, if the normalized `C mod p` has full row rank, the p-local cokernel of E has cyclic factors `p^{k_i}` and the p-adic valuation of its **maximal-minor determinantal divisor** is exactly `F=Σk_i`. This elementary claim is [P: paper proof, NOT separately Lean checked]. Crucially the residual metric `C B C^T` can remain singular mod p, and its valuation must not be inferred from rank alone. The checked finite Rust pilot has `rank(C mod3)=8` and `det(C B C^T)≡2`, showing no universal extra residual gain. Any higher-dimensional, hyperbolic or sheaf/geometric lift remains [H/S] unless it produces a testable new inequality. Independent actual-field semantics #50 and global same-E rank transport #51 remain open. The historical failed Lean runs for Phase135 remain preserved; the [repaired whole-root check passed](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/actions/runs/41138437037) and [PR #53 was merged](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/pull/53) with no new field cutoff.

## Phase136: a sharper conditional arithmetic guard

[Phase136MinorSaturation.lean](lean/TenthDiscriminant/Phase136MinorSaturation.lean) now checks the exact formula for a selected maximal minor of the SAME integer E under its integral row extraction. If a selected minor of C is not divisible by nonzero p, the original selected minor has **exactly** p^F and not p^(F+1). ## Phase137: a bounded new conjecture, not further polishing of the old cutoff

The [integer Chebyshev moving-frame protocol](research/radial_chebyshev/PHASE137_PROTOCOL.md) draws on [Tao's Gram-covolume account](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/19/a-sketch-of-the-proof-of-non-existence-of-siegel-zeroes/) while preserving the identical original integer observation E, complete ellipse Q, ordinary jets, B and inert-prime divisor. The [exact finite Rust check](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/actions/runs/41179952647) reports 12 of 32 original-jet diagonals improved and no worsened rows; it also shows the stronger *pointwise* version is false. A new [H] conjecture seeks uniform derivative-aware normalized saving >=43/10000 for D>=46b, enough (conditional on a new log bracket) for a strict 46b bound by the existing arithmetic lower. This is **not checked**, and if large-j derivative growth destroys the gain, the registered negative result should be retained. Issue #51 needs exact global same-E transport; issue #50 still needs outside mathematical reconstruction.

**Conjecture-refutation update (31 Oct):** The discrete Sobolev diagonal of the original (2,2) jet row under integer constant shifts is the exact quadratic `666616+6960c+42c²`, with unique integer minimizer c=-83 rather than Chebyshev c=-86. [Five-test Rust CI](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/actions/runs/41180605701) checks the finite instance. Thus even the enhanced Chebyshev frame is not optimal for the genuine *ordinary derivative* metric. The [Sobolev closest-vector reformulation](research/radial_chebyshev/PHASE137_PROTOCOL.md) records the elementary completion-of-squares proof and a falsifiable uniform cost question. Independently, [Hales-inspired linchpin review surfaces](research/tight_radial_v1/LINCHPIN_AND_REGISTRY_20261031.md) differentiate the actual class-number-one field statement, the same-Gram arithmetic exponent, global positivity and the final rational certificate; the [Palomar Registry](https://palomar-registry.org/statement) is a mechanical-record standard, not referee endorsement, and [Hexagon](https://hexagonmath.org/) is a dissemination venue, not a correctness gate. ## Phase138: radial-fiber block volumes and a non-scalar candidate bridge

The [graded Gram-volume study](research/graded_covolume/PHASE138_FIBER_VOLUME.md) replaces scalar-by-scalar Hadamard optimization with intrinsic determinants of higher-dimensional radial fibers indexed by r-s, on the **identical** integer ordinary-jet E. These block determinants survive every monic integral radial moving frame; Hadamard–Fischer provides a nested, monotone hierarchy of upper bounds. [Existing formal central-symmetry parity](lean/TenthDiscriminant/CentralSymmetry.lean) eliminates all opposite-parity couplings. A concrete [32-row exact integer test](research/graded_covolume/phase138_exact_fibers.py) exposes significant finite block savings and an instructive negative result: using a coefficient optimal for one row across all rows worsens the full diagonal bound. The next [H] is a **uniform, analytically certifiable block or canonical-correlation estimate** through every production derivative order, not a claim of new field cutoff. The [October CMSA summit report](https://cmsa.fas.harvard.edu/media/2026/10/Summit-on-Math-Research-in-the-Age-of-AI.pdf) provides the explicit distinction between novel conjectures, reusable repositories and semantic proof validation; it does not verify our conjectures. External field reconstruction #50 and global same-E proof compatibility #51 remain open.
