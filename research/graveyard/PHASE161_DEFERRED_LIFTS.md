# Graveyard — Phase161 ideas not promoted

This file is deliberately a **short decision record**, not another research log. It preserves why attractive vocabulary or hypotheses were not installed as production mathematics. Historical discussion remains visible in [issue #78](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant/issues/78); the [reader-facing surviving results](../reverse_determinant/PHASE161_SYMMETRY_GUIDE.md) live outside this graveyard.

**Retired [R]: symmetry forces the \(p-1\)-root rank certificate.** False. A centered conjugation/negation-stable support at \(D=3,p=11,S=3,\rho=5\) has eleven distinct effective roots and original rank \(30\). Delete the centre and rank falls to \(29\); its parity obstruction is \(\operatorname{diag}(0,3)\) modulo 11. The general claim of automatic deletion is closed. What remains true is *conditional* block nonvanishing, recorded in the reader guide.

**Not promoted [S/H]: a Spin or Pin improvement to the field bound.** An orientation-reversing parity map at \(p=31,S=5\) is an ordinary quadratic symmetry, but no useful integral Clifford lift, new source-compatible valuation, or analytic improvement has been constructed. **Pin is not a magic cure for an unsuccessful Spin analogy.** Promote a future lift only after specifying the quadratic space, exact base ring, orientation, isometry, faithful action, new invariant and a matched no-Clifford control.

**Not promoted [S/H]: neural identity, neurite growth or raw intermanifold port as a theorem.** Source-compatible cokernel transport and gluing were already defined in [Phase160](../reverse_determinant/PHASE160_ARITHMETIC_NEURITES.md). No additional identity ecology, sheaf/gerbe cocycle or inter-\(D\) equivalence is now licensed. Such a construction requires explicit domains and arrows, a recoverable remainder, and evidence of mathematical advantage beyond those exact module maps.

**Not promoted [H]: automatic \(5\)-billion cutoff from the symmetry.** The uniform original-jet positivity bridge, lower-bound composition, production-radius support construction and same-Gram analytic upper have not been discharged by these finite cases. The existing \(47\)-billion theorem is unaffected.

**To revive an entry:** show a precise, source-matched statement, its failed/null control, a concrete improvement not expressible by existing module/Gram objects, and relevant Lean or independent mathematical verification. Otherwise keep it here. A name, dimension lift or appealing analogy is not itself evidence.
