## Formal object, not a geometric slogan

For p=5 **inert** in K=Q(sqrt(-D)) with maximal order O=Z[omega], omega²-omega+m=0 and m=(D+1)/4, O/pO=F25. Fix actual full maximal-order Q closed under conjugation, ORIGINAL monomial rows and ORIGINAL ordinary Z-jets. For a column (j,z), E_i(j,z)∈O/p. Let P_tau be the actual involutive permutation (j,z)↦(j,conj(z)). Every original integer-coefficient monomial/ordinary jet, and every integral Frobenius source-row shear or exact p-division, obeys

    bar(E) = E P_tau

at every integral level where the division is defined. The original Hermitian trace Gram after reduction is

    G = Tr_{F25/F5}( E bar(E)^T ) = 2 E P_tau E^T  (mod5).

The middle H=E P_tau E^T is fixed under bar, hence has entries in F5. This is a **conjugate-Hermitian cross pairing on a finite extension**, not a same-side Euclidean dot product and not physical quantum computation.

Let k=F25, n=source-row count. The source rank rE=rank_k(E), Gram rank rG=rank_F5(G), and row-space radical delta=rE-rG. The source evaluation kernel dimension is n-rE and total Gram kernel is n-rG; the 2 contributions **must not be identified or added twice**. The equality follows from the induced nondegenerate ambient permutation pairing and the quotient by ker(E); this is classical linear algebra over F25.
