2026-11-13. Proposed **after** the order1/2/4/11 exact CI evidence. Not a prior-to-discovery preregistration. This file is frozen BEFORE its executable model and tests.

Let O be any Dedekind domain and I an invertible nonprincipal ideal of finite class order m. Let N be a positive multiple of m. Define M_N=direct_sum_{i=1}^N I, with the declared filtration F_k=direct_sum_{i=1}^k I, 0<=k<=N.

**External theorem:** by Steinitz classification, the unfiltered projective module M_N is free, because its determinant class is [I]^N=1. This does not identify M_N with the repository's original integer-row source.

**New categorical discrimination theorem [P-paper target]:** There is NO filtered O-linear isomorphism between (M_N,F) and (O^N,F_std), where F_std is the standard coordinate flag. Any such isomorphism would induce isomorphisms gr_k F≈I≈O=gr_k F_std for every k, contradicting nonprincipality of I. Thus the forgetful functor from filtered projectives to projectives is not conservative enough to recognize named ideal-line transports.

The associated-graded functor records the ordered family ([I],...,[I]) in Pic(O)^N, and is retained as a typed remainder when forgetting the filtration. This is not a claim that the particular entries of the original Hermitian trace Gram determine this flag.
