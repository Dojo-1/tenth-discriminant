
The internal Smith COLUMN transforms are for computing e ONLY and are never transported to original B. The last term is counted ONLY after every source e is resolved and rank(Cprim modp)=n. Under censored levels it is UNKNOWN and the old plus secondary lower is the only safe bound. The formula is classical [P-paper]; Phase292's numbers are original-source finite observations [M], NOT a new uniform theorem.
