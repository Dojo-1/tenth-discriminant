# Phase368 — Polycosmic adversarial research bandit (H)

**Preregistered design date:** 20 November 2026.  **Parent:** Phase367 draft PR #278, already focused and full-root Lean checked at `9a958cee96077a805ce18412e1e0bc3b7095b09d`.  **Status:** proposed research allocation algorithm, not mathematical evidence for a stronger discriminant bound.

## Actual question

Can a typed, constrained, multiobjective Worldlet bandit allocate a finite investigation budget among bounded Prolog refutations, Lean proof obligations, arithmetic source audits and genuinely independent review, while preserving the *same original integer trace Gram*, not double counting correlated tests, and not granting its own authority to promote mathematical claims?

## Frozen source and authority boundary

The active field research uses the Phase367 original integer-pair coordinate source and original ordinary Z-jets holding conjugate W fixed. The exact source identity is `G=2*(AA^T + D BB^T)` on ALL `(j,q)` columns. It does **not** use a padded Gram, a selected minor, or the D203 maximal-order fixture from Phase306. The Phase306 geometry is a useful external **contrast**, with nonzero `cross_coefficient_category` remainder; default selection excludes this contrast arm. Evaluation is scoped by source ID AND formal claim scope; a finite toy may refute a universal matrix assertion but cannot establish a field-301 theorem.

## Active mathematical objective

**Falsify or locate the smallest missing premise in the 301^4 source-to-field research programme, not optimize the cutoff directly.** The bandit never chooses truth. Its choices are suggestions requiring separate execution; no GitHub, Lean, Prolog, external-review or repository operation is launched by the Python policy.

## Worldlets, clocks and operations

Eight typed Worldlets, eight candidate research arms and five genuine **three-Worldlet** hyperedges. Worldlets have distinct carriers (integer-pair lattice source; Lean dependency DAG; finite Prolog search; p-adic filtration; field chart; independent reviewer; Phase306 D203 contrast; governance). Three clocks: fast bounded falsification, mesoscopic compilation/source checks, slow independent review. Required operators: ergocetic exploration = candidate selection, erdodetic integration = receipt-based evidence recording, deweave = retain claim and source provenance with typed remainder. Hypercomplex numeric coupling is NOT a proven benefit and is excluded from the minimal controller.

## Policy (experimental heuristic)

Maintain two empirical Bernoulli axes **diagnostic yield** and **discharged local obligation**, plus a separate task cost. Estimate optimistic confidence scores per arm; choose a non-dominated Pareto arm, after an explicit once-per-eligible-arm coverage sweep. A cost constraint, typed source-compatibility gate, and clock permissions apply BEFORE selection. This is a Pareto-UCB-like engineering heuristic, not an implementation of an existing regret-optimal algorithm. The posted code does not have a regret proof. Arm outcomes are correlated, sources change and human review is delayed; any textbook i.i.d. assumption is unsafe.

No scalar measures 'intelligence' or theorem correctness. A candidate may be viable on one metric and poor on another; the Pareto frontier is retained. Change point / slow review clocks are represented, but Bayesian nonstationarity detection and dynamic topology learning are **not** implemented in Phase368.

## External outcome governance

The Python allocator accepts `Receipt` objects only if they exactly match a separately supplied approved receipt registry; it cannot authorize or authenticate that registry. In production, validation must use an external CI/human-verification adapter with independent credentials and append-only provenance. `fixture` receipts used by unit tests are synthetic and have no authority. Prolog checks cannot close encoded Lean theorems; Lean checks do not close semantic field-chart review. **There is no evidence-level promotion function** in the allocator. The Phase368 Prolog file is a separate test of source, validator and transport rules, not independent validation of the Python implementation or of arithmetic.

## Falsifiers

1. Any D203 maximal-order source is silently treated as the original arbitrary-D integer-pair source.
2. A derivative clock increase is treated as a unimodular row-frame gauge and its p-adic valuation is transported unchanged.
3. A model-authored or failed CI receipt changes its own P/H/M evidence class, or a missing success marker is ignored.
4. A finite Prolog test discharges a field-301 class-number assertion.
5. The three-Worldlet hyperedge can be satisfied with two Worldlets.
6. The multiobjective geometry is a decorative relabeling of a single scalar UCB policy, with no measurable discriminator over a compute-matched baseline.

## Experiment plan and stop rule

First gate: `python3 -m unittest discover -s research/phase368 -p 'test_*.py' -v`; GNU Prolog finite governance checks must emit the exact `PHASE368_GUARD_PASS` marker, and source-anchor guard from Phase365 must pass. These test **type/authority gates**, not performance.

Second gate, still H: freeze a held-out corpus of real GitHub/Lean/Prolog research obstructions with externally checked validation records and fixed outcome costs; compare allocation against uniform round-robin, cost-aware flat UCB, and ablations of source gating, typed remainder, triadic hyperedges, and distinct clocks. Run at least 20 paired seeds, fixed CPU budget and independent held-out audit. Report valid falsifiers/CPU minute, unclosed/repeated tasks, false promotion attempts, source-mismatch rate, semantic-review coverage, and interval estimates. No gain until this corpus is acquired and evaluated by an independent witness.

Stop if the source gate ever fails, false-promotions exceed zero, the synthetic benchmark is mistaken for external evaluation, or compute-matched flat baselines reproduce all benefits. In that case keep the evidence ledger and retire the extra architecture rather than present a spurious performance claim.
