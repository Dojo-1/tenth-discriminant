## The concrete Phase291–292 measurements

**D563, original 700-row source.** The primitive evaluation rank is 700; the primitive Hermitian trace Gram rank is 684, with corank 16. The depth-three primitive Smith profile is
[
{0:684,;1:15,;2:1},
]
so the primitive residual (5)-valuation is exactly 17. The original Gram valuation is 3497, decomposing as the already measured 3480 source contribution plus this finite residual 17. This is a single-field, single-source calculation.

**D587, full 730-row source.** The primitive Hermitian Gram rank is 694, corank 36, with primitive residual valuation 36; the full original Gram valuation is 3924. The 725-row matched diagnostic gives 3855. The five added rows contribute a finite Schur determinant valuation of 69, split into sector contributions 28 and 41.

**D583/D587 transport control.** The local field comparison is a square-unit isometry, but it does not preserve the original point support: 104 original points are lost under the mod-5 transport, with an explicit pointwise counterexample. Therefore the two original source Grams cannot be identified through that local isometry.

**Hostile Schur control.** An integral positive-metric counterworld reverses the apparent monotonicity: the old determinant has (5)-valuation 1 while the extended determinant has valuation 0. The observed 69 is therefore not promoted to a general monotone (p)-local Schur theorem.

## Evidence labels

- **P:** exact algebraic identities and finite-dimensional statements under explicit hypotheses.
- **M:** bounded original-source computations, Smith profiles and adversarial controls after the exact-head workflows.
- **H:** any uniform large-discriminant class-number-one valuation, any new same-Gram arithmetic lower bound, or any improved field cutoff.
- **R:** adding index and pairing defects, transferring local field isometries to original source support, or promoting a finite Schur gain to a monotone theorem.
