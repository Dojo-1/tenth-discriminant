## T1: observation inclusion does not transport p-adic valuation

Original trace-2 real-point pilot, rows 1,Z, J=1, R=2, S=1:
- Q1={(1,0),(4,0)} -> G1=[[4,10],[10,34]], det=36, v_3=2.
- Q2=Q1 union {(2,0)} -> G2=[[6,14],[14,42]], det=56, v_3=0.
- G2-G1=[[2,4],[4,8]]=2 (1,2)^T(1,2) is PSD. The Gram determinant increases although the 3-adic determinant valuation falls.

Status: **M pass; R** for the naive inference "Q subset Q' implies each v_p(det G(Q)) <= v_p(det G(Q'))". This toy intentionally does NOT satisfy the class-number-one/inertness prerequisites.

## T2: partial determinantal divisors preserve additional information

Let p=3, e=(0,1,2), Lambda=diag(1,3,9), K=[[2,1,0],[1,2,1],[0,1,2]], U=[[1,1,0],[0,1,1],[0,0,1]] (det U=1), H=Lambda K Lambda, and G=U^{-1} H (U^{-1})^T.

Exact output:
- G=[[122,-123,135],[-123,126,-135],[135,-135,162]].
- det K=4, det G=2916=4*3^6.
- delta_1(G)=1, delta_2(G)=27, delta_3(G)=2916.
- v_3(delta_k)=(0,3,6), while the elementary lower required by the diagonal factorization is 2*tau_k=(0,2,6), tau=(0,1,3).
- The whole delta profile is identical before/after the integral frame U (Cauchy–Binet invariance).

Status: **M pass**. The k=2 component is nontrivial and not determined by the numerical value of det alone. This is an arbitrary integral SPD pilot, **not** a quadratic-field ordinary-jet sample Gram and **not** evidence of a new discriminant cutoff.

## T3: small rectangular pilot predicts little partial-minor gain

R=4,S=2,p=3, e_(r,s)=min(floor(r/p), S-1-s), row-major exponents (0,0,0,0,0,0,1,0). Sorted prefix tau_k=(0,0,0,0,0,0,0,1).

Status: **M pass**. The generic partial-minor bound is trivial for k<8 in this pilot; do NOT advertise improvements from min-k divisibility for small rectangles. Any richer method must demonstrate additional structure beyond this exponent floor.

## T4: real determinant-one frames need not preserve arithmetic objects

Take U=diag(2,1/2), G=I_2. det U=1 and det(UGU^T)=1, yet UGU^T=diag(4,1/4), not an integral matrix. Integer determinantal divisors cannot be applied to that representation.

Status: **M pass; R** for unqualified "SL_n(R) moving frames preserve integer arithmetic profile". Their geometry is legitimate only with a separate lattice/transport structure.
